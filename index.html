About

Built by a PM who uses this every day

I'm a Senior Product Manager with experience across large-scale retail and e-commerce products. Everything in JupiSun Academy comes from my actual workflow — prompts I use weekly, agents I built to save hours of repetitive work, and frameworks I developed after learning what actually works vs what sounds good in theory.

I started JupiSun Academy because I couldn't find a PM-specific AI course that went beyond "here's how to use ChatGPT." The prompts, the agents, the research workflows — those all needed to be built from scratch. So I built them, documented them, and turned them into courses.

The 55 prompts in the PM OS are the ones I actually use. The frameworks in the courses are ones I've tested on real product problems. Nothing is made up for the sake of filling a module.
